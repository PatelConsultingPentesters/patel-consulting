# Image credits

No third-party photographs are used in this version. The hero is an original inline SVG; the Open Graph card is generated.

If you add photography later, record here for each file: original source URL, creator, license (Pexels/Unsplash), date checked, and a check for identifiable people, logos and trademarks. Convert to WebP/AVIF, max ~1600px wide, under ~150 KB, and add `loading="lazy"` plus `width`/`height`.
